The lens stack

Five layers between you and the glare.

A finished lens is a sandwich of coatings around a high-index core. The green-violet sheen you see on good glasses is the anti-reflective layer doing its job.

L-01

Anti-reflective coating

Cancels reflections on both surfaces, so people see your eyes instead of the café lights behind you.

L-02

Blue-light filter

Trims the harshest band of screen light without turning the world yellow.

L-03

Scratch-resistant hard coat

A quartz-hard shell over the optics. Keys, bags, and toddlers included in the warranty maths.

L-04

UV barrier

Blocks UVA and UVB across the whole lens, clear or tinted — Malaysian sun is not optional.

L-05

High-index core, 1.67

The prescription itself, cut thin. Strong corrections without the bottle-bottom look.
