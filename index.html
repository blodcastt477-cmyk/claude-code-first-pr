Become a Member Our Businesses

A diversified cooperative built on integrity, innovation, and empowerment — growing entrepreneurs across every sector it touches.

Koperasi Usahawan Bukit Bintang Berhad governs and grows a portfolio of member businesses from the heart of one of Kuala Lumpur's busiest commercial districts. Headquartered at Ilham Tower, the cooperative combines regulatory rigor with entrepreneurial ambition.

Under its board's stewardship, KUBINTANG has diversified across oilfield services, property development, F&B franchising, trading, events, and digital marketing — creating opportunities for members across multiple industries rather than a single trade.
