Dato’ Dr. Sofiya Mohd Rozhi

Chairman — Koperasi Usahawan Bukit Bintang Berhad

Dato’ Dr. Sofiya Mohd Rozhi has been serving as the Chairman of Koperasi Usahawan Bukit Bintang Berhad since 2022. She is an entrepreneur, visionary leader, and corporate strategist with over 15 years of experience in business development, organizational leadership, entrepreneurship, and the oil & gas industry.

She is recognized for her expertise in Oil & Gas Industry Equipment, Supply Chain Management, Business Development, and Strategic Partnerships. Under her leadership, she has contributed to initiatives focused on industrial growth, operational excellence, and sustainable business expansion.

As Chairman, she provides strategic direction and governance to ensure the cooperative’s continued growth, sustainability, and long-term success — empowering entrepreneurs, fostering innovation, and creating opportunities across multiple industries.

Core Business Activities

Five divisions. One ambition.

01

Oil & Gas Supply

Offshore · Marine · Aviation Logistics

Supplying offshore oil rigs with industrial drilling equipment, bulk liquids, specialized chemicals, and essential consumables. Delivery support includes marine vessels and helicopter logistics.

Drilling Equipment

Heavy-duty drill bits, casings, BOP systems, and mud pumps delivered on schedule.

Bulk Liquids

Diesel fuel, drilling mud, cement slurry, and freshwater transported using dedicated supply vessels.

Logistics & Transport

Marine vessel scheduling, helicopter crew transfers, and real-time cargo tracking.

Our Services

Registration, licensing & compliance — handled.

KUBINTANG provides professional assistance for contractors, companies, and business owners in managing their registration, licensing, and compliance needs. Our services are designed to help you save time, reduce paperwork, and ensure your application process is handled smoothly.

SV/01

Contractor License Registration

CIDB Contractor License Registration from G1 to G7 for contractors who want to register or upgrade their contractor grade.

SV/02

CIDB Related Registration

Registration assistance for MCORE, SKOR, and SPKK, helping contractors meet industry requirements and improve business credibility.

SV/03

MOF License Registration

Ministry of Finance MOF license registration for businesses that wish to participate in government procurement and tender opportunities.

SV/04

SPAN Permit Registration

Assistance with SPAN permit registration for companies involved in water supply and sewerage-related services.

SV/05

ISO Registration

Support for ISO registration to help businesses improve quality standards, management systems, and company professionalism.

SV/06

CIDB Green Card Registration

Registration assistance for CIDB Green Card for workers and contractors involved in construction-related work.

SV/07

Company Registration

We assist with the registration of Sdn Bhd companies for business owners who want to operate under a proper corporate structure.

SV/08

TNB Vendor Registration

Assistance with TNB vendor registration for companies that wish to become registered vendors or suppliers.

SV/09

Integrity Course Registration

Registration assistance for Integrity Courses required for certain licensing, tender, and compliance purposes.

SV/10

Business Management Course Registration

Assistance with Business Management Course (SKP) registration for business owners and contractors who need to fulfil industry requirements.

SV/11

TM Technical Certificate Registration

Registration assistance for TM Technical Certificate for businesses involved in technical and telecommunication-related services.
