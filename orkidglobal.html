About the Cooperative

Rooted in members. Reaching further.

Koperasi Orkid Global Berhad is a member-owned Malaysian cooperative built on a simple belief: that people achieve more together than alone. By pooling the savings, skills and ambition of our members, we build and invest in enterprises that generate lasting returns and shared prosperity.

Like the orchid we take our name from, we are patient and resilient — growing steadily, blooming where others cannot, and returning value to the community that nurtures us.

Our Vision

To be a cooperative where every member’s future can flourish.

We envision a thriving cooperative that stands as a trusted vehicle for shared wealth in Malaysia — one that grows responsibly, invests wisely, and lifts the economic wellbeing of its members and their communities.

Our Mission

Pool strength, invest with care, share the harvest.

To mobilise the collective resources of our members into sound, diversified ventures — managed with integrity and returned to members through dividends, opportunity and support that compounds over time.
